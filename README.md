## Featured Projects

### NYC TaxiGuard
> **Big Data** | Team Leader

Real-time taxi demand and anomaly detection pipeline. Spark for batch analysis over ~4 million trips, Kafka + Flink for real-time demand windows and basic anomaly detection on a simulated stream of ~1,000 trips/minute, and a live Streamlit dashboard with an interactive taxi-zone map.

- **Scale:** ~4M trips · ~1,000 trips/min (simulated)
- **Stack:** `HDFS` · `Spark` · `Kafka` · `Flink` · `Streamlit`
- [View Repository](https://github.com/joseph-emadd/NYC-Taxi-Big-Data)

---

### Football Player & Referee Detection and Tracking
> **Computer Vision** | Team Leader

YOLO11n trained for 100 epochs at 640x640 to detect players, goalkeepers and referees (label validation, 80/20 train/validation split), with ByteTrack tracking them across match video and producing tracked output videos.

- **Stack:** `YOLO11` · `ByteTrack` · `PyTorch` · `OpenCV`
- [View Repository](https://github.com/joseph-emadd/Player-And-Refree-Detection)

---

### Student Performance Prediction
> **Machine Learning** | Kaggle

Compared 16 regression models, from Linear, Ridge and Lasso to XGBoost, LightGBM and CatBoost. Linear Regression scored best (R² = 0.988, MAE = 1.65).

- **Stack:** `Python` · `Scikit-learn` · `XGBoost` · `LightGBM` · `CatBoost`
- [View on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/code/josephemadd/student-performance-prediction-regression)
